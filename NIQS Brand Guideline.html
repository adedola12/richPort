About This Guide
This document defines the complete visual and verbal identity of the Nigerian Institute of Quantity Surveyors. It governs every official touchpoint — print, digital, stationery, and communication.
Adherence to this guideline is mandatory for all NIQS national secretariat communications, state chapter materials, and any third-party work produced on behalf of the Institute.

A Message from
the President

"The Nigerian Institute of Quantity Surveyors remains committed to producing world-class professionals who will drive the sustainable development of our nation's built environment. Our brand is more than a mark — it is a covenant with the public, with our members, and with the profession itself."

This guideline codifies the visual language and communication standards of NIQS. It exists to ensure that every touchpoint — from a member's letterhead to a chapter banner to a social media post — speaks with one consistent, authoritative voice.

The NIQS brand is built on 56 years of professional leadership. It is heraldic in origin, editorial in its modern expression, and civic in its purpose. This guide preserves those qualities while providing the practical specifications needed to apply them correctly in every medium.

For brand usage enquiries, contact the NIQS National Secretariat. Requests to use the NIQS name, logo, or brand assets in third-party materials require written approval.

Who We Are

Nigeria's Premier Professional Body
for Quantity Surveyors

The Nigerian Institute of Quantity Surveyors (NIQS) is the foremost professional and regulatory body for quantity surveyors in Nigeria. Founded in 1969 by Nigerian professionals trained in the United Kingdom, the Institute was formally registered under the Land Perpetual Succession Act in 1970 and subsequently established by Decree No. 31 of 5 December 1986 — now codified as CAP Q1, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria (LFN) 2004.

NIQS regulates and advances quantity surveying practice across all sectors of Nigeria's built environment — from cost planning and procurement to contract administration and value management. With over 10,000 members, 4,000+ corporate-qualified practitioners, and chapters in all 36 states and the FCT, the Institute is the authoritative voice of the construction cost management profession in Nigeria.

Professional Leadership

NIQS is founded by Nigerian quantity surveyors trained in the United Kingdom, recognising the urgent need for an indigenous professional body alongside the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors.

2021

The Profession

The Construction
Cost Managers

A quantity surveyor is a construction professional with expert knowledge of building economics, procurement, and contract law. They manage all financial and contractual aspects of a project from earliest conception through to final account — ensuring client objectives are met within agreed budgets.

The work of a quantity surveyor spans every stage of a capital project: feasibility estimates, cost plans, bills of quantities, procurement strategy, tender management, contract administration, valuations, variations, claims resolution, and post-contract auditing.

Voice & Tone

How NIQS Writes

1. Third-Person Institutional
NIQS refers to itself in the third person in all formal communications. "NIQS administers…", "The Institute regulates…". The word "we" is reserved for the most informal touchpoints only.
2. Declarative, Not Persuasive
NIQS does not sell. It announces, informs, and regulates. Sentences state facts. Avoid "powerful", "amazing", "transformative". Avoid exclamation marks.
✗ AVOID
"Exciting news! NIQS is thrilled to announce our amazing new CPD programme!"
✓ USE
"NIQS announces the commencement of the 2025 CPD programme."
3. Precise & Quantified
Anchor all claims in verifiable numbers. Use NIQS provenance figures consistently: 10,000+ members · 4,000+ corporate · 37 chapters · 56 years · 15+ reciprocity agreements. Do not invent. Do not round up.

Official Emblem

NIQS Mark

The NIQS emblem is a heraldic eagle rendered in gold, set above the navy roundel, framed by a gold heraldic ring. The emblem is the distilled mark of the institution — it carries the full weight of NIQS's 56-year heritage.

Light-BG Variant
Use on white or off-white surfaces — letterheads, certificates, document headers. The emblem appears on a clean white backplate.
Dark-BG Variant
The navy backplate is baked in. Use on coloured backgrounds, dark surfaces, navbar tiles, or anywhere a self-contained emblem is needed.
Rule
Do not redraw, recolour, or composite alternates. Always use one of the six provided official files.

Logo Protection

Clear Space & Minimum Size

The minimum clear space around any NIQS logo is equal to the height of the letter "N" in the wordmark, on all four sides. No text, graphics, photography, or other elements may intrude into this zone.

Correct Usage

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do
Use on approved backgrounds only. White, off-white (#F6F7FB), or official navy (#000066). For any other background, confirm contrast and use the appropriate variant.
✓ Do
Use SVG for all digital applications. Use PNG from the NIQS Logo/PNG/ folder when SVG is not supported. Never use a JPEG — compression introduces artefacts.
✗ Don't
Do not recolour the logo. The NIQS logo must never appear in any colour other than its official variants. No single-colour, reversed, or gradient treatments.
✗ Don't
Do not add effects. No drop shadows, glows, gradients, emboss, or bevel applied to the logo file. The mark stands on its own authority.
✗ Don't
Do not stretch or distort. Scale proportionally from any corner. Never compress or stretch the lockup in any direction.

Colour Rules

How to Apply
the Palette

1
Navy is the dominant colour
On any given communication, navy should account for ~60% of pigment. Gold is used sparingly — never as a fill for large surfaces.
2
Gold is an accent, not a background
Never fill a large surface with solid Gold. Use --gold-wash for tag fills and hover states.
3
Sections alternate for rhythm
Alternate between White and Off-1 (#F6F7FB) across sections. Never place two navy sections in sequence without a light section between.
4
Text on navy is always white or gold
--fg-inverse (#FFFFFF) for body. Gold for eyebrows and accents. Never use dark body text on navy.
Semantic Colours

Rule: Semantic colours are functional only. They appear in tabular data, status indicators, and system notifications. Never in marketing materials or editorial content.

Signature Visual

The Five-Image Fan Strip

The NIQS hero section features five rectangular photographs arranged in a tilted fan — a signature visual device unique to the brand. Images are sized to ramp from smallest on the outer edges to largest in the centre, and rotated slightly (−2.5°, −1°, 0°, +1°, +2.5°) to create a fanned, dimensional effect.

This device communicates the breadth of NIQS membership — different faces, different project types, different geographies — brought together under one professional roof.

Rule
This device must appear on the home page hero only. Do not use it on interior sub-pages or printed materials without explicit approval. Images must be real NIQS photography in production — not stock imagery.

Language to Avoid

What NIQS Never Says

✗ Avoid✓ Use Instead
"We're excited to announce…""NIQS announces…"
"Amazing new CPD!""New CPD programme 2025"
"Empowering professionals""Advancing the QS profession"
"Nigeria's leading body""Nigeria's foremost body"
"Click here""View details" / "Apply now"
"Game-changing!"State the fact instead
"Transforming the industry""Driving professionalism in…"
Any exclamation mark!Remove. State the fact.
Approved Headlines

Headlines That Sound Like NIQS

All display headlines follow the same structure: 2–3 lines, one key noun in Gold. The Gold word is always the primary noun. Headlines state — they do not sell.

Advancing Nigeria's Built Environment
Professional Services & Standards
Setting the Gold Standard
55 Years Shaping Nigeria's Built Environment
Ready to Join NIQS?
Choose Your Membership
Headline Checklist
  • □Is the primary noun in Gold?
  • □Tracking set to −0.03em to −0.04em?
  • □Does it declare, not sell?
  • □Title Case throughout?
  • □One gold word only — not two?
