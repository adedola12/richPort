What Quiv is — in one paragraph

Quiv is a purpose-built quantity surveying software suite designed for Nigerian and African QS practitioners. It replaces the manual process of measuring construction quantities from drawings — currently done with scale rules, paper, and Excel — with a structured, digital, offline-first desktop application. Fragment 1 (Quiv Measure) handles PDF/DWG/Revit drawing import and on-screen measurement. Fragment 2 (Quiv Cost) generates BESMM-compliant Bills of Quantities. The full suite covers the entire QS workflow from first measurement to final account. Competing software costs NGN 4.5M+ per year. Quiv is priced at NGN 15,000–35,000/month.

Why this is Richard's most important project

Richard is a trained Quantity Surveyor who became a designer. He is designing a product for his own profession — which means he has genuine domain expertise that no other designer working on this problem would have without years of study. The product has a full PRD (v1 and v2), market research report, design specification, and working interactive HTML prototypes. The v2 pivot (from Revit plugin to standalone app) came from real market research — Nigerian practitioners can't afford Revit at NGN 4.5M/year. That's a real product decision made for a real business reason. That's what senior portfolios show.

Richard's role on Quiv

Creative Lead, ADLM Studio. Building Quiv alongside the founder. Not officially a co-founder, but leading all design — UX, UI, brand system, prototypes, design specifications. The two HTML prototype files (Quiv_Prototype_v2.html and Quiv_Measure_v4.html) and the Design Specification document are Richard's direct design output.

The v1 → v2 pivot — why it matters for the portfolio

PRD v1.0 (February 2026) designed Quiv as an Autodesk Revit plugin. Market research conducted in March 2026 found that Revit costs NGN 4.6M/year per user — making it inaccessible to over 99% of the Nigerian QS market, which predominantly works with PDF drawings. The product was fundamentally rearchitected as a standalone Electron.js desktop app that reads PDF, DWG, RVT, and IFC files without requiring any third-party software. This pivot is a portfolio story in itself: Richard was part of a team that built something, found a fundamental problem with it through research, and made the hard call to start over correctly. That's senior-level thinking.

The two prototypes — what they are

Quiv_Prototype_v2.html — The original Revit plugin UI. A panel that lives inside Autodesk Revit's interface. Shows project management, element selection, takeoff workflow, and calculation screens. Design system: navy + blue (#1E90D6) + orange accent, Outfit font.

Quiv_Measure_v4.html — The standalone app. Hub dashboard, PDF measurement canvas, takeoff list panel, project library, sign-in. Built for African QS practitioners working from PDF drawings. Tagline visible in prototype: "Built for African quantity surveyors. Measure, price, and tender — without Revit, without AutoCAD, without the dollar bill."
